Laura’s story

“The world is yours.”

Laura Lara grew up in El Paso, the daughter of immigrant parents from Mexico who sacrificed everything for their children’s future.

Portrait of Laura
from Lala Blush
  1. The classroom

    Seven years teaching high school business, and on the path to principal. “Five years ago, I was a high school teacher making $39,000 a year.”

  2. The question

    Her students kept asking: you make us do projects about building businesses, but you’ve never built one. During the pandemic, she listened.

  3. Lala Blush

    She discovered paramedical tattooing and built Lala Blush around paramedical tattooing, skin restoration and confidence driven aesthetics.

  4. Why it matters to her

    “I firsthand saw how insecurities from acne scars, surgical scars, aging, and simply not feeling like yourself could affect the way people show up in the world.”

  5. Soleyah

    Her fifth location and her biggest, in Sunset Valley, because she did not see a med spa there that offered everything or felt welcoming.

“Anything you’re envisioning in your head, envision it 10 times bigger, because it will happen.”

Laura Lara, to What Now Austin, August 2026
