The founder

From a $39,000 classroom to her fifth location.

Laura spent seven years teaching high school business. Her students kept asking why she taught them to build businesses but had never built one. During the pandemic she listened, found paramedical tattooing, and started Lala Blush.

Soleyah is her biggest location yet, and it was designed from day one to grow into a brand people will call an Austin original.
